## The 25 mm grid

Knobs sit on **25 mm centres**, which is what lets a pot bank and an encoder
bank stand side by side on one panel with their shafts in line. Both bank
boards are the same 20 × 100 mm outline for the same reason, and the two single
boards are a 25 mm square — one cell of the same grid.

The two banks differ in one detail worth knowing before you drill: the pot
bank's shafts sit 1.53 mm further along the board than the encoder bank's. Knob
spacing is identical; the whole row is offset.

Buttons are the exception. The button bank puts its four switches on **12.5 mm
centres**, with an LED 7 mm to the side of each — a button does not need a
knob's clearance, and a row of four fits in 75 mm instead of 100.

:::caution[Two bushing sizes, two hole sizes]
Pots use **M9 bushings**, so their panel holes are Ø9.5 mm. Encoders use **M7**,
Ø7.5 mm — and that thread is still to be confirmed against Bourns' datasheet.
Drill for the board you are mounting.
:::

Pots and encoders both present 15 mm of shaft above their mounting face, so
knobs sit at the same height across a mixed panel.

## How boards mount

**Satellites hang from the panel.** The pots or encoders are through-hole on the
top side and the board hangs on their bushings and nuts. Everything else on
those boards is surface-mount on the underside, which also keeps assembly
single-sided.

Two cases need a standoff as well:

- **The single boards** carry one bushing each, so an **M2 standoff** beside it
  stops the board turning when the nut goes on or the knob is forced past its
  stop. 10 mm for the pot, 6.5 mm for the encoder — the heights of their
  mounting faces.
- **The button bank** has no bushings at all, and pressing a button pushes the
  board away from the panel, so **two M2 standoffs** carry it. Their length goes
  with the switch height and the caps, which are not chosen yet.

**Everything else sits on printed parts** on the enclosure floor:

- a **rear rail** carrying the back panel and every rear-I/O daughterboard —
  power, audio jacks, MIDI;
- a **motherboard frame**, which can go anywhere on the floor.

Daughterboards mount on M3 × 8 mm standoffs. Details and the printable parts are
in `hardware/mounting/README.md`.

:::note[Defined, not yet fitted]
The parts were defined on 13 October 2026, when the condition for revisiting them
was "once every daughterboard is drawn" — which has since happened. Nothing has been
test-fitted against a physical board, so treat the dimensions as settled and the
arrangement as untested.
:::
