Everything here describes **Rev B**, the version the campaign ships. It is written
for the person deciding whether to build on this platform, which means it leads
with the connectors, the contracts and the dimensions rather than the pitch.

:::caution[No Rev B board has been built yet]
The motherboard and all six daughterboards are drawn, routed and uploaded to JLC
for a first run of five of each, so these designs are final — but they have not
been ordered yet, and none has been assembled or powered on. **Nothing on these pages has been
measured on a Rev B board.** Where a number was proven on the Rev A prototype it
says so, and [the prototype page](/prototype/) covers what that hardware actually
demonstrated.
:::

## What is not here yet

The firmware side lives in [rodeo-sdk](/sdk/). Enclosure drawings, a panel
template and the Rev B bring-up guide are still to be written — they wait on
boards to bring up.
