Everything here describes **Rev B**, the version the campaign ships. It is written
for the person deciding whether to build on this platform, which means it leads
with the connectors, the contracts and the dimensions rather than the pitch.

:::caution[No Rev B board has been fabricated]
The motherboard's schematic is in progress and the daughterboards are drawn and
routed with fab outputs waiting. Nothing on these pages has been measured on a
Rev B board, because none exists yet. Where a number was proven on the Rev A
prototype it says so, and [the prototype page](/prototype/) covers what that
hardware actually demonstrated.
:::

## What is not here yet

The firmware side lives in [rodeo-sdk](/sdk/). Enclosure drawings, a panel
template and the Rev B bring-up guide are still to be written — they wait on
boards to bring up.
