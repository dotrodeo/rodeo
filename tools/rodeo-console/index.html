The SD card

card

Put the Rodeo in disk mode — hold encoders 1 and 4 for a second, or press d on its serial port — and the card mounts like any other drive. Open it here and this page will read the library the way the firmware will, convert whatever you drop in, and tell you what the Rodeo would quietly skip.

This browser has no File System Access API, so the card cannot be opened in place. Chrome or Edge can. Everything still works; changes come back as a zip to copy across.

Eject the drive when you are done. The Rodeo re-reads the card on the way back, and the serial port and USB-MIDI come back with it.
